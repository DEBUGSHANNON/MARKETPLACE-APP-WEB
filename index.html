CampusTrade UTM

CampusTrade UTM is an interactive student-to-student campus marketplace for Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM Skudai). Visitors can publicly browse campus items, textbooks, electronics, mobility gear, and student services without logging in.

Verified @graduate.utm.my students may optionally sign in with Google to publish listings, save items to a wishlist, and arrange safe handovers at UTM campus meetup zones.
