CampusTrade UTM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

CampusTrade UTM is a student-to-student campus marketplace built for the Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM Skudai) community. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you sign in and use the application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account & UTM Verification Data: When you sign in using Google OAuth via Firebase Authentication, we receive your name, verified UTM student email address (@graduate.utm.my), and basic profile identifier.
  • Optional Campus Profile Details: Residential college, dorm pickup area, and contact phone number if you choose to add them to your profile.
  • Marketplace Activity: Listings you publish, saved wishlist items, handover reservations (including selected UTM meetup locations and times), and direct chat messages with buyers or sellers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Verify that marketplace participants belong to the @graduate.utm.my campus community.
  • Display your seller or buyer display name on listings, reservations, and campus chat threads.
  • Store your saved wishlist items and coordinate safe on-campus handovers at UTM Skudai.

3. Data Storage & Security

Application data is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication with strict role-based security rules. Private profile details, wishlists, reservations, and chat messages are restricted to the authorized account owner or transaction participants. We do not sell or share your personal information with third-party advertisers.

4. Your Choices & Contact

You may update your profile details, remove your available listings, or remove items from your wishlist at any time inside CampusTrade UTM. For questions or data removal requests, contact the app administrator at lamwaishan@graduate.utm.my.
