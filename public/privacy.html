Privacy Policy for CampusTrade UTM

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · Last Updated: November 23, 2026

Overview: CampusTrade UTM (accessible at https://debugshannon.github.io/MARKETPLACE-APP-WEB/) is a student-to-student campus marketplace built for Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM Skudai). Anyone can publicly browse marketplace listings without signing in. Sign-in via Google is only required when a verified UTM student chooses to publish listings, save a wishlist, reserve items, or message another student to coordinate an on-campus handover.

1. Who We Are

CampusTrade UTM is an independent campus web application designed to help UTM Skudai students buy, sell, and exchange pre-loved textbooks, electronics, mobility gear, and student essentials safely at recognized campus meetup zones. For privacy inquiries, contact the application maintainer at lamwaishan@graduate.utm.my.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required to operate marketplace features:

  • Google Sign-In / OAuth Profile Data (Basic Scopes Only): When you choose to sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication, we request only basic sign-in identity scopes (openid, email, and profile). From Google, we receive your unique user identifier, verified student email address (@graduate.utm.my), and display name. We do not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar, Contacts, or any sensitive or restricted Google Workspace scopes.
  • Optional Campus Profile Information: Inside the Personal Profile view, you may optionally provide your residential college (e.g., Kolej Tun Razak, Kolej Rahman Putra), preferred campus pickup block, and contact phone number.
  • Marketplace Listings, Reservations & Messages: Item titles, descriptions, prices in MYR, campus pickup zones, uploaded or preset item images, saved wishlist references, handover meetup proposals (UTM zone, date, time, and optional coordinates), and direct buyer–seller chat messages created by you.
  • Technical & Local Browser Storage: Firebase Authentication session tokens and essential browser storage used strictly to keep you signed in and support offline/network recovery. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral ad cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Campus Eligibility Verification: Confirm that sellers and buyers creating or reserving listings hold a verified @graduate.utm.my email address.
  • Marketplace Functionality: Publish your item listings, display your chosen display name to transaction counterparts, store your private wishlist, and coordinate safe face-to-face handovers at UTM Skudai.
  • Direct Buyer–Seller Coordination: Deliver chat messages and handover schedule updates between the specific buyer and seller involved in a reservation.
  • Optional AI Listing Assistance: When you explicitly click an AI helper tool (such as drafting a listing description or asking the Campus Finder), only the item text or query you submit is processed to return marketplace suggestions. Your Google account credentials and private chat histories are never shared with advertisers.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

CampusTrade UTM's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used exclusively to provide user-facing authentication and campus marketplace features within CampusTrade UTM.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade Google user data or personal student information to third parties.
  • We never use Google user data for targeted advertising, marketing profiling, or data brokering.
  • We do not allow human reading of user data unless explicitly authorized by you for support or required for security/legal compliance.

5. Data Storage, Sharing & Security

User accounts and marketplace records are hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. Access is enforced through strict Firestore Security Rules:

  • Public Listings: Published listing titles, prices, categories, conditions, and campus pickup zones are publicly visible so visitors can explore the marketplace before signing in.
  • Private Data: Personal profile fields (phone number, dorm address), private wishlists, handover reservations, and reservation chat threads are restricted solely to the authenticated account owner or the two transaction participants.

6. Data Retention & Your Privacy Rights

We retain your profile and marketplace records only while your account or listings remain active:

  • You can edit your profile details, update or delete your available listings, and remove items from your wishlist directly inside CampusTrade UTM at any time.
  • You may revoke CampusTrade UTM's Google Sign-In access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.
  • To request complete deletion of your CampusTrade UTM account profile, listings, or stored messages, email lamwaishan@graduate.utm.my from your registered @graduate.utm.my address. Verified deletion requests are processed within 7 days.

7. Eligibility & Changes to This Policy

CampusTrade UTM is intended for university students and adults (18+) at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia and is not directed at children under 13. If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above and publish the updated policy at this URL.
